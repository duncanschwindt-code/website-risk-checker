# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users;
- protecting teenagers, website owners, contributors, and reporters from privacy harm.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- sexualized language or imagery, unwanted attention, or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- unsupported accusations of scams, fraud, crimes, or malicious intent;
- intimidation or retaliation against a reporter, website owner, contributor, or maintainer;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards and may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful. They will communicate moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in
public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
`[PRIVATE COMMUNITY CONTACT]`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Community leaders must
respect reporter privacy and safety.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders may apply these consequences:

1. **Correction:** A private written warning and explanation.
2. **Warning:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior and a period of no interaction
   with involved people.
3. **Temporary ban:** A time-limited ban from interaction or public communication.
4. **Permanent ban:** Permanent removal for sustained patterns, harassment, aggression, or other
   serious violations.
